Screen panel repair

A branch, a dog, a ball, or just twelve Florida summers. We cut the old spline out, fit new mesh tight enough that there are no ripples, and match the colour of what is there. Most single panels are done the day we arrive.

Complete pool cage rescreen

When more panels are torn than whole, it is cheaper to do the lot. Every panel, every screw and every anchor gets looked at. A two-story cage takes three or four days; a single story is usually two.

Full rescreen, in detail

Lanai and porch screens

Lanais, screened porches and patio rooms. We rescreen walls and ceilings, replace sagging roof panels after a leak, and fit privacy or no-see-um mesh where you need it. A standard lanai is a one-day job.

Screen doors and hardware

Doors that drag, slam or will not latch. New handles, latches, closers and bug sweeps, or a whole new door. We also cut in pet doors so the dog stops going through the mesh.

Anchors, bolts and roof panels

Rusted anchor bolts are what fail first in a storm. We replace them in the same hole, not a new one, and tighten the cage back down. Roof panels are measured and fitted from the walk boards.

Screen guide

Which mesh, what it costs on top, and when it is worth it. Prices are per panel, typical, and confirmed in your written quote.

Mesh

Standard fibreglass, 18×14includedWhat most Florida cages have. Best airflow, lasts seven to ten years in the sun. Charcoal, to match.
No-see-um, 20×20+ $6A tighter weave that stops the tiny biting midges. Slightly less breeze. Good on roofs and near pasture or water.
Pet screen+ $15Vinyl-coated polyester, about three times as tough as standard. Low panels and screen doors where the dog pushes through.
Florida Glass+ $12Laminated mesh for privacy and to keep mower clippings and dust out. Lower panels only, never on a roof.

Good to know

Do I need to be home? No. Most repairs happen outside. Leave the gate open, and we leave the invoice on the door or text it to you.

How fast can you come? Single panels and doors are often the same day or the next. A full rescreen is usually scheduled within the week.

Repair or rescreen the whole cage? If more than a third of the panels are torn or brittle, a full rescreen is cheaper per panel and the mesh will all age together. Jerry will tell you honestly which it is.

Do you build new enclosures? No. We repair and rescreen existing cages, lanais and porches, and replace doors, hardware and anchors. For a new cage we can point you to a builder.

What mesh do you use? Standard 18×14 fibreglass in charcoal unless you ask for no-see-um, pet screen or Florida Glass. The guide above has the differences.

Is there a trip charge? Not in East Hillsborough. Outside that, Jerry will say so in the quote before anyone drives.

How do I pay? On completion, against the written invoice. Nothing up front to hold a date.

What about after a storm? Text photos as soon as it is safe. We work the list in order and tell you where you are on it. Loose panels and rusted anchors are worth checking in spring, before the season.

Can you match my existing screen? Yes. Charcoal is standard; we carry the common colours and can order the rest.

Do you do window screens? Ask. For a house full of them, yes. For a single window we may suggest a hardware shop will do it cheaper.
