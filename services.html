Screen panel repair

A branch, a dog, a ball, or twelve Florida summers. We cut the old spline out, fit new mesh tight enough that there are no ripples, and match the colour of what is already there. Most single panels are done the day we arrive, and you do not need to be home.

  • Wall, roof and door panels, any size
  • Charcoal 18×14 fibreglass as standard; no-see-um, pet screen and Florida Glass on request
  • New spline every time, never re-used
  • Loose neighbouring panels retightened while we are there

Typical: wall panels from $45, roof panels from $75

Complete pool cage rescreen

When more panels are torn than whole, it is cheaper to do the lot and the mesh will all age together. Every panel comes out, every screw and anchor gets looked at, and the frame is cleaned before the new mesh goes on. A single story is usually two days; a two-story cage takes three or four.

  • Every panel, walls and roof
  • Screws replaced throughout; rusted anchors swapped in the same hole
  • Pressure wash of the frame while the mesh is off, on request
  • Doors rescreened and rehung with new sweeps

Typical: from $1,200 for a single-story cage, priced as one job

Lanai and porch screens

Lanais, screened porches and patio rooms. We rescreen walls and ceilings, replace roof panels that have sagged after a leak, and fit privacy or no-see-um mesh where you need it. If the roof has been holding water, say so when you send the photos and we bring the right panels.

  • Walls, ceilings and kick plates
  • Sagging or water-filled roof panels replaced
  • Privacy mesh and Florida Glass on the neighbour side
  • Screened-in porch built onto an existing roof, by quote

Typical: a standard lanai rescreen is a one-day job

Screen doors and hardware

Doors that drag, slam or will not latch. New handles, latches, closers and bug sweeps, or a whole new door hung square. We also cut pet doors into the screen door or a low panel so the dog stops going through the mesh.

  • Rescreen with new bug sweep
  • Handles, latches, closers, hinges
  • New aluminium screen doors, hung and adjusted
  • Pet doors in the door or a low panel

Typical: door rescreen from $95, handle and latch set from $65, pet door from $140

Anchors, bolts and roof panels

Rusted anchor bolts are what fail first in a storm, and most cages over fifteen years old have a few. We replace them in the same hole with stainless, not a new hole next to it, and tighten the cage back down. Roof panels are measured from above and fitted from the walk boards.

  • Anchor and tapcon replacement, same hole
  • Loose or missing screws throughout the frame
  • Roof panels of any size, fitted from walk boards
  • A pre-season check before June, by request

Typical: anchor bolts from $12 each, fitted
